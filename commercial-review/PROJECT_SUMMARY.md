**Campus Navigation** is an independent, student-developed mobile app by Leo Alavi and Mohammad Raouf Abedini, Macquarie students. It helps students, staff and visitors find their way around the North Ryde campus. It began in March 2026 as our COMP3130 Mobile App Development major project, and we have continued developing it since.

Macquarie's published campus map is clear and widely used. As a static PDF, though, it cannot show where you are, search for a building code, or guide you to a room. The app uses the University's published campus map, unaltered, as its base, and adds an interactive layer that we built ourselves:

- a GPS calibration that shows your live position on the map;
- a searchable database of 161 campus locations with codes and common names;
- walking directions drawn on the map, with step-by-step instructions and arrival detection (route paths are calculated by Google and OpenRouteService);
- 360° panoramic photos of building entrances and lecture theatres;
- safety and public-transport information.

We later adapted the same codebase for an Open Day 2026 visitor companion. We also built a separate app for Astronomy Open Night 2026, now published on the App Store. ⟦CONFIRM: "used by attendees on the night"⟧

Campus Navigation is not an official University product. Because the base map, including its crest, is University material, we are asking for the University's guidance and permission on map use, attribution, branding, distribution and any non-commercial conditions, and we will follow whatever the University requires.
