**What the app is**
Campus Navigation is an independent, student-developed mobile app that Raouf Abedini and I have built to help students, staff and visitors find their way around campus. It takes the University's published campus map and makes it interactive. The app shows your live location on the map, lets you search 161 campus locations by name or building code, draws walking directions on the map with step-by-step instructions, and includes 360° panoramic photos so people can see a building's entrance or lecture theatre before they arrive.

**What is ours and what belongs to the University**
To be clear about the boundary: the base map in the app is the University's published campus map. It is used unaltered, and it still includes the University's crest and wordmark (one attachment shows this). - the app itself;
- the GPS calibration;
- the location database;
- search;
- the route display and instructions;
- arrival detection;
- the 360° photography.

Route paths themselves are calculated by Google and OpenRouteService. The app is not an official University product, and we have been careful not to present it as one.

## Should you include a demo video?

**Not with the first email. Send screenshots now and offer a demo.** Reasons:
- The first readers are the Commercial Officer and possibly brand or legal staff. They need to see the boundary (University map vs your work) at a glance. Stills do that better.
- A screen recording would show the crest and University map in motion, plus Google's map, and can be forwarded or reused with less control.
- A video is easy to send once someone asks.

If they ask for one, record **60–90 seconds** on a real phone or the simulator:
1. Open the app to the campus map; the blue dot appears (5 s).
2. Search "18WW" or "library"; select a result (10 s).
3. Start walking directions; show the route line and the step panel (20 s).
4. Open "Explore in 360°" for Macquarie Theatre; pan once and tap the hotspot (20 s).
5. Settings → About, holding on the independence notice (10 s).

Share it as a **private cloud link** (OneDrive with your MQ account is the most natural) rather than an attachment. That keeps the email small and lets you revoke access.
