# Questions for Macquarie (Commercial Officer and other stakeholders)

Grouped so they can be answered in one reply. Choose the 4–6 most important for the first email (marked ★). Keep the rest for a meeting or follow-up.

## Map and branding
1. ★ May we continue to use the University's published campus map as the base layer of the app? If so, on what conditions?
2. ★ Should the University crest and wordmark that appear on the published map remain, be masked, or be removed from the in-app copy?
3. Does the same answer apply to the four overlay maps (parking, accessibility, permits, water)?
4. Is formal written permission or a licence required for the map assets, and who issues it?
5. ★ What attribution wording should appear in the app and store listings (for example "Campus map © Macquarie University, used with permission")?
6. What disclaimer wording does the University require (for example "not an official Macquarie University app")?
7. Are there University brand guidelines we must follow, or colours and names we should avoid (including "MQ" in our app identifiers and web domain)?
8. Would the University prefer that we redraw an independent base map instead? If so, is there anything we may not reproduce (building footprints, grid references)?

## Data
9. Is there an approved, more up-to-date campus map or location data source we should use instead?
10. Is there an API or data feed for campus locations, buildings or maps?
11. May we use building names, addresses and short descriptions of University facilities?
12. Would the University want to review location data for accuracy before a public release?

## Distribution and use
13. ★ May the app be distributed publicly, including on the Apple App Store and Google Play?
14. ★ Is non-commercial use a requirement? If so, what counts as commercial (e.g. donations, a future paid tier, sponsorship)?
15. Would the University be willing to promote or link to the app (orientation, Open Day, the visitor web pages)? If so, through whom?
16. Does the University need to review updates or new features (e.g. a future Open Day mode) before release?
17. Are there conditions for using the app at University events (Open Day, Astronomy Open Night), and who approves those?
18. Our source code is currently public on GitHub, including the map images. Should we make those repositories private until permission is settled?

## Ownership and IP
19. ★ The project began as our COMP3130 major project. Does the University have any ownership or IP interest in work that began as coursework?
20. Does the student IP policy apply to work done after the unit ended (from June 2026 onward)?
21. Can we (as student developers, working alongside our Syllabus Sync project) keep ownership of the code and functionality we created independently, while using the University's map assets under licence?
22. For the Astronomy Open Night app, the App Store listing credits the "Astronomy Night – FSE Outreach Team" for copyright. Is there a University position on ownership of that app we should be aware of? *(Ask only if you are comfortable raising it; see `SEND_CHECKLIST.md` §I.)*

## Pathway and contacts
23. Is there a commercialisation, partnership or licensing pathway if the University would like to support wider adoption?
24. Is the Incubator the right channel for any future commercial discussion, or is there another process?
25. ★ Who should be our formal contact for approvals and future questions?
26. What are the next steps and an approximate timeframe, so we can plan around our final semester?
