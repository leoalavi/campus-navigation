# What belongs to Macquarie or third parties, and what we built

This is an engineering inventory, not legal advice. Its purpose is to show the University exactly where its material sits in the app, so that permissions can be given or refused one asset at a time.

We added nothing to the image itself | **High. Permission needed.** This is the core University asset. The image is used unaltered, so do not call it "transformed". |

## Summary of the boundary in one line

> The University's published campus map and overlays are the base image. Everything interactive on top of that image (location data and calibration, search, live position, route display and instructions, arrival detection, compass, panorama tours and the app itself) was built by the two of us. Route paths are calculated by third-party routing services.
