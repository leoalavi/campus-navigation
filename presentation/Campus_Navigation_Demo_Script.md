# Campus Navigation — Live Demo Script

About 2–3 minutes. Laptop (macOS app). Short sentences. Speak slowly.

Before you start: the app is already open on the **Home** screen,
onboarding is finished, and the laptop is on Wi-Fi.
See `Campus_Navigation_Demo_Readiness.md` → "Before the audience arrives".

---

## 1. Introduction (about 20 seconds)

[Show the Home screen]

> Hello everyone. This is Campus Navigation.
>
> It helps students find buildings at Macquarie University.
>
> You can search for a place, see where it is, and get walking directions.

## 2. The campus map (about 20 seconds)

[Click **Navigation** at the bottom]

> This is our campus map.
>
> It shows the buildings, the main walkways, and useful places like food and parking.

[Point to the category buttons at the top: Student Services, Faculty, Food & Drink, Parking]

> These buttons help you browse by category.

## 3. My real location (about 25 seconds)

[Click the **locate button** — the red round button, bottom right]

> Now I will show my real location.
>
> The app uses the location from this laptop.

[Wait for the blue dot. If it appears, point to it.]

> This dot is where I am standing right now.

*If the dot does not appear within about 10 seconds, say:*

> Location can be slow indoors. I will continue with the search, and come back to it.

## 4. Search for a building (about 25 seconds)

[Click the **search bar**. Type: `service connect`]

> Let's find a place. I will search for "Service Connect".
>
> Students go here for help with enrolment, IT, and student cards.

[Click the result **18 Wally's Walk (Service Connect / IT / Learning Support)**]

[Click **View in Campus Map**]

## 5. Building details (about 20 seconds)

[Point to the panel at the bottom]

> Here are the details.
>
> The name, the building code — 18WW — and a short description.
>
> The map moves to show exactly where the building is.

## 6. Route to the building (about 35 seconds)

[Click the **search bar** again. Type: `service connect`. Click the same result.]

[Click **Navigate with Google Maps**]

> Now I will ask for a walking route from where I am.

[Wait for the route line to appear]

> The line shows the way to walk.
>
> Here we can see the distance and the walking time.

[Point to **Walk** — it is selected]

> We can also choose other travel modes, but on campus, walking is best.

*If you see a red message instead of a route, say:*

> The route needs internet and my location. The connection here is slow,
> so I will show the rest of the app instead.

[Then go to step 7.]

## 7. Finish (about 20 seconds)

[Click the **X** on the building panel, then click **Home**]

> To finish: Campus Navigation helps new students and visitors
> find their way around campus more easily.
>
> It is designed to connect with Syllabus Sync. A link can open a building
> directly in Campus Navigation.
>
> Thank you. I am happy to answer any questions.

---

## Backup lines (use only if needed)

**GPS does not work:**
> Location is not available right now. The search and the campus map
> still work, so let me show you those.

**Internet does not work:**
> The internet is slow here. The campus map and building search work
> without internet. Routes need a connection.

**The app closes or freezes:**
> Let me restart the app. It takes a few seconds.

[In the terminal press `q`, then run `./scripts/run.sh macos` again.
While it loads, keep talking about the purpose of the app.]

**Someone asks "Does it work offline?"**
> The campus map and the building list are stored in the app.
> Routes and live train times need the internet.

**Someone asks about accessibility:**
> Accessibility details are still being checked. We do not show them
> as verified information yet.
